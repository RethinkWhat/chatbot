1. What is the LLM Program of Saint Louis University? The LLM 
Program of Saint Louis University is an advanced post-graduate degree 
program in law. It is a 48-unit non-thesis post-graduate course, delivered 
purely online. 
 
2. When will the LLM Program of Saint Louis University School 
of Law be opened? The program will begin in August 2024. 
 
3. Who are qualified to enroll in the LLM Program? All who 
graduated with the LL.B. or J.D. degree. Passing the Bar is not a 
requirement. 
 
4. Can enrollment be done online? The enrollment process will be 
conducted online, but the original paper documents must be submitted in 
person or by mail to the Office of the Dean, Rm. 414 Diego Silang Building, 
Saint Louis University, Bonifacio Road, Baguio City. 
 
5. When will classes and enrollment begin? Classes in the LLM 
program shall officially begin at the same time as classes in SLU begin. 
Enrolment begins at least a week before the start of classes. 
 
6. What is the modality and schedule of classes? Classes will be held 
online and will meet one Saturday and Sunday per month. The classes will 
be from August to December and the second semester would begin January 
of the following year and end in June.

7. How long is the program? The SLU LL.M. program may be finished in 
two years. You may enroll in a maximum of twelve units per semester. 
 
8. Is a thesis a requirement to graduate from the LL.M. Program 
of Saint Louis University? No, we are offering a non-thesis program, 
but papers and exams will be required for the satisfactory completion of 
every subject.
